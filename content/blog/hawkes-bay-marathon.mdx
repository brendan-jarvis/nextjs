I used Claude Code to plan the Hawke's Bay marathon. The plan lived in Notion. It had the weeks, the long runs, the flights, and the Airbnb. On 16 May 2026 I ran the ASICS Runaway Hawke's Bay Marathon, bib 479, and finished in 3:20:03.

That was 151st of 1,127, 136th of 695 men, and 36th of 140 in the 30–34 category. Net time, not gun time. The result is public.

The plan was a document, not a coach. Claude Code took the training I was already logging and wrote a week, then wrote the trip around the race. Notion was the thing I actually followed. It did not message me on the morning, and it did not pick the pace at 30 km.

The first 5 km went through in 20:10, about 4:02 per kilometre. By 21 km the average was 4:34. From 37 to 40 km the split slowed to 5:27, then the last 2.2 km came back to 4:28. The clock stopped at 3:20:03, about 4:44 per kilometre for the whole race. I had gone out faster than I finished.

I would hand it the week structure again, and the travel. Flights and an Airbnb are a list, and a list is what it is good at. I would not hand it the last 10 km. That was a pace I had to run, not a pace a document could hold.

The result page is on SportSplits, under bib 479.
